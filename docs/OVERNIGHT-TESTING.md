# v0.29 — Agreed batch gameplay checklist

Public upload and publication were explicitly approved on November 1, 2026. This checklist applies to v0.29 after its GitHub Pages deployment completes.

1. Confirm v0.29 in the header. Load an existing park: balance and existing construction should remain intact.
2. Test 45° seating and both end-stair mirrors using CHUNK-2.8-TESTING.md. Hover a stair to see the exact required path tile and failure reason. Inspect visible concourses at all four camera angles.
3. Open gates. Fans originate in the parking lot and come in waves. Same-color parties should sit in adjacent seats in a row. They prefer proximity to the action and space from strangers when possible. A full stadium will fill gaps; central stair seats may hold solo fans.
4. Expand TEAM COLORS & FINISHES. Change primary/secondary/accent. Linked chairs, padded walls, shop awnings, scoreboard trim and street banners should change. Choose a custom item color, click Apply, then click an object. Change the team palette again: that object should retain its custom color. Enable Follow team colors and apply to relink it. Undo and reload should preserve the intended colors.
5. Switch the outfield fence between chain link and team-color padding. Build walls using each finish, rotate them, save/reload and verify fan routes remain blocked by walls.
6. Place/rotate food, drinks, ice cream and souvenir shops; place all four scoreboards. They should have distinct appearances, cost the shown amounts, accept color overrides and survive reload. Displays are placeholders; shops do not generate sales yet.
7. Inspect parking, houses, gas station, sports bar, apartments and banner lamps. They are scenery, with no traffic/interiors/economy. Wait for the game to end: all fans should return to parking. 100 admissions should add exactly $500; departures should add nothing.
8. Export/import the park. New object types, palette, overrides, material, elevations and money should survive. Use Undo to verify construction refunds and color restoration.

Known limits: square concourses meet diagonal stair endpoints at corners. Direct row connections require matching facing. Gate paths that double back across the fixed entrance approach may stall opposing fan streams; this remains a separate issue. No children, mascot, needs meters, attraction visits, singing or wave animations are implemented in this build.
