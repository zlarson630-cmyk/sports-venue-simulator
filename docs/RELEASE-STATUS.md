# Release status — v0.39

Bathroom needs, a deeper outfield and tighter foul corners are the active pass. The user's two-level park is stored as tests/fixtures/two-level-wedge-v038.json. Upper wedge ring/offset metadata now reaches fan seat calculations; dropping it had produced six invalid positions and prevented postgame departures.

Fans have a 0–100 bathroom meter. Click a fan during play or use Inspect a fan. Needs increase, and seated fans at 75 seek a reachable ground-level restroom. Each restroom has two stalls, a FIFO interior queue and at most four visitors in flight. Fans are hidden while queued/using inside the building, then return to the same reserved seat. Party members act independently. Narrow row/stair corridors admit one restroom visitor at a time; visitors on separate routes can move together. Trips start after initial arrivals finish, in bounded batches. Once a batch starts returning, the next batch waits until those fans are seated. This avoids opposing turns at shared restroom doorways in the first needs prototype; after the clock ends, visitors already away from their seats take a validated route straight to the gate. Restrooms need an adjoining connected ground walkway; building a restroom on an upper deck is not supported yet. No reachable restroom is explicitly reported. Needs reset for each game.

The shared seat-access tree remains the path to ground. Restroom routing uses only the ground walkway graph beyond that point, avoiding conflicting shortcuts through seating rows. Movement uses physical spacing for restroom trips; other arrival/departure collision rules remain. Broad jitter/rendering cleanup is still queued; do not claim all jitter is resolved.

Center field is exactly 400 feet from home along the center ray, with unchanged 300-foot foul poles. The outer diagonal boundary moves out to retain about 50 feet of clearance. For the last 100 feet approaching each foul pole, the foul fence lies 10 feet outside the line, then angles back to the unchanged dugout/stand fence. Concave field polygons are triangulated once for footprint protection. Revision 7 migration fully refunds old pieces swallowed by the expanded field and removes their independent fillers; other construction is preserved. The reported current park migrates unchanged. The diagonal demo is relocated beyond the new wall.
