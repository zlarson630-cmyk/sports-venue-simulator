# Release status — v0.40

Food concessions and a floating Inspect Fan panel are the active pass. Hunger grows independently of bathroom need. Hungry seated fans visit connected ground-level concessions after arrivals finish, queue inside, buy a $5 meal after six seconds, and return to the same seat. One server per stand, at most four visitors in flight. Admission and food revenue are separate; both persist and survive construction undo. Food and bathroom trips share ground-route preparation, corridor reservations and bounded return batches. No new need or routing calculations are added to canvas drawing.

At clock zero, stop starting food service. A current purchase finishes before exit flow begins; queued and travelling customers leave without buying. End Game remains an immediate manual stop. Inspect Fan (F), canvas clicks during play or the picker open the floating inspector; X/Escape close it. Reports hunger, bathroom need, party, seat, activity and completed visits, including missing reachable food or restrooms.

Existing limitations: amenities require adjoining connected ground walkway; shops on upper decks, configurable prices, drinks, stock and staffing are not implemented. Existing crowd jitter and expensive static-world rendering still need a dedicated profiling pass. Solid concrete gaps, stair/concourse cosmetic joins, player-assigned level visibility, eight camera angles, bulk paths and whole-piece red delete previews remain queued.
