        Game day operations

        Based on recorded guest experiences and simulated events. Mentions count individual reactions, not a survey of every attendee.

        Build and run your first game

        1. Build a small ballpark

          Choose a path tool, then click or tap tiles to lay a continuous walkway from the main gate into the park. Place a bleacher next to the walkway. If you build raised seating, connect the walkway to the bottom of its stairs. Use Rotate to turn a piece before placing it and Undo to take back your last build.

          Want a ready-made example? In Game Day, choose Build demo route to add a walkway and bleacher if the area is clear.

        2. Choose your crowd and game length

          In Game Day, start with 100 fans and 2 minutes. The crowd is capped by the number of seats fans can reach. Changes made during play apply to the next game.

        3. Open the gates

          Click Open gates to start. Fans arrive in groups, pay $5 each, and head to their seats. When the timer ends, watch them leave. Use End game if you want to stop early.
